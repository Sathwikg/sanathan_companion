3. Your Consent and How to Withdraw It

We use your personal data because you agree to this notice when you create your account. Optional uses, such as usage statistics, are asked for separately and stay off until you switch them on.

  • Switch an optional use off at any time in My Profile, under Privacy and Your Data. It stops at once.
  • Withdraw all consent by deleting your account in My Profile. It takes one step, just as agreeing did.
  • Withdrawing does not affect what was done lawfully before, and every consent you give or withdraw is recorded so you can see it in your data download.

4. Phone Permissions

  • Location: only while the app is open and only when you tap My Location. You can refuse and choose a region instead.
  • Notifications: for the reminders and alerts you choose. Turn each type off in the app, or all of them in your phone settings.
  • Start After Restart: so your reminders keep working after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: to let you feel each japa count.
  • Camera, Photos and Microphone: asked only when you save a wallpaper, or when an administrator uploads a deity image or chant audio. We never scan your photo library or record without you starting it.

5. Who Else Handles Your Data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Only these service providers process it for us, under contract and only to run the app:

  • Our hosting provider: runs the servers and database where your account and practice are stored.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, if you choose it, and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to your phone using its notification token.

We disclose personal data to a government agency only when Indian law requires it.

6. Advertising

The app shows no advertisements and contains no advertising or tracking software. If that ever changes, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first. We will never track or target advertising at anyone under 18.

7. How We Protect It

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. On the phone, your sign-in is kept in the secure storage of Android or iOS.
  • Access inside our team is limited by role, and access to the system is logged.
  • If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay through the app (a notification and your inbox) and by email where needed, with what happened, its likely effect and what you can do. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India and to CERT-In as the law requires.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: download everything we hold about you from My Profile, under Download My Data.
  • Correction: change your details in My Profile, or ask us to correct anything else.
  • Erasure: delete your account in My Profile. Your account, practice, favourites, notifications, devices, feedback and consent history are erased at once. Security logs are kept until their one-year period ends, because the law requires it.
  • Nomination: name a person in My Profile who may use these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Grievance Redressal: write to us (section 12 below) or send Feedback with the type Privacy / My Data Request. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaint to the Board: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

In return, the law asks you to give true details and not to file false or frivolous complaints.

9. Adults Only

Accounts are only for people aged 18 or older, and you confirm your age when you sign up. Under Indian law a child is anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process a child's personal data; if we learn that an account belongs to a child, we delete it. Please tell us if you believe this has happened.

10. Changes to This Notice

Each version of this notice has a number and a date (above). When we change what we collect or why, we raise the version and ask you to read and accept it again in the app before you continue.
