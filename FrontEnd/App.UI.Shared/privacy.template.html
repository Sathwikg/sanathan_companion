3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

When you use the mobile application, certain device features may request your permission:

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Requested solely to provide hyper-local sunrise, sunset, and planetary timings for the Vedic Panchangam. You may choose to deny this permission and instead manually pick a region.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver your daily sadhana reminders, prayer alerts, auspicious tithi notifications, and festival announcements. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in app or system settings.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Alarms on Android devices reset whenever your device restarts. This permission allows the app to restore your scheduled daily sadhana alarms after your phone reboots.
  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Used solely if you choose to take a photo of a deity or select a wallpaper from your device gallery. We do not inspect other photos in your library.
  • Microphone: Used exclusively if you record chant recitation or stotra audio within the app.

5. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We implement multi-layered defenses to ensure your information is safe:

  • Transport Encryption: All client-to-server traffic is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) standards.
  • Secure Storage on Device: Mobile credentials and tokens are safeguarded using platform hardware keychains (EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android and Apple Keychain on iOS).
  • No Plain-Text Passwords: Credentials cannot be read by anyone, including our system administrators.
  • Token Lifetime & Revocation: Sessions support instant invalidation upon password reset or logout.
